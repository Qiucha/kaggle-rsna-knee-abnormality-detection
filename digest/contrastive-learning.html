Contrastive learning pipeline

A paired knee-MRI/report pipeline that trains, checkpoints, resumes and evaluates on the full 4,407-study cohort. The levers have now been measured against gold pathology labels: depth of image-tower adaptation is the only one with a replicated effect. Report language, schedule length and text-tower adaptation are all null. The later four-tower LM-head comparison scored all 58 gold cases, including two from the original test split; the full test cohort has not been evaluated.

Bottom line. The software trains, and something real is being learned: adapting four of DINOv2's twelve blocks beats one at three seeds with every interval excluding zero, and a shuffled-pairing control loses to the true pairing. The older four-block label numbers below are development statistics: 56 of the 58 gold studies were training pairs or sat in the selection split, and their reports state the findings. That question now has an answer. A held-out replication retrained the encoder with the gold studies excluded and re-probed on studies it had never seen: the pooled exposed level of 0.7106 is inflated by −0.0168 of macro AUROC (−0.0163 by the data-size-matched route), leaving a generalisation estimate of 0.6938. The three levels subtract exactly: 0.6438 unadapted, 0.6938 unexposed, 0.7106 exposed, and 4-block adaptation still beats unadapted DINOv2 by +0.0500 with all three seed intervals excluding zero. A data-size control put the confound at −0.0005. So about a quarter of the headline gain was exposure and three quarters is real. Quote 0.6938 beside 0.7105, never instead of it — they are different numbers about different things. See the held-out write-up.
New 58-case LM-head comparison. Four gold-holdout tower checkpoints were fitted with the same three-seed, 12-label head trained on LM verdicts. Macro AUROC was 0.7659 for DINOv2-small/E5-base, 0.7541 for small/small, 0.7504 for base/base and 0.7605 for base/small. All 58 gold cases were excluded from head fitting and selection. Both size-mismatched pairings lead the matched pairing with the same image size, but all four paired size intervals include zero. Linear controls scored 0.7312–0.7362, below every early-stopped MLP; they showed far less late validation-loss rebound. See the full settings matrix, loss curves and per-label AUROC or Markdown record. The older 56-case gold-only OOF AUROC and newer 58-case LM-head AUROC use different supervision and cannot be read as a paired gain.

What moves the number, and what does not

Pooled out-of-fold macro AUROC over 56 gold studies × 12 labels, uniform_mean_768 features — CLS ⊕ patch-mean, 2 × 384, not a base-model hidden width. Unadapted DINOv2-small reads these labels at 0.6438. Full detail and every interval: the variants write-up and the curves page.

Measured on a different scale, so not in the table above. Adapting the text tower's final block was also null, but that matrix was scored on mean validation Recall@1 over 441 candidates on the quarantined translated cohort — not on the gold probe, which it never went through. On that scale it returned −0.7936 pp across three matched seeds (SD 1.0816, interval spanning zero), against +1.2094 pp for the matching image-only arm. The two numbers are comparable to each other and to nothing else on this page. Frozen multilingual E5 remains the reference.

Two confounds a reader will raise. Every arm used --lr-image 1e-5, chosen when one block was trainable, so “depth helps” and “this learning rate suits more blocks” are not yet separated. And the shuffled-pairing control earned its share of the gain by memorising the random pairings, which is instance discrimination rather than domain adaptation — so the objective/adaptation split is an ordering, not a clean decomposition.

Full path to complete contrastive learning

✓

1. Audit pairing, preprocessing, reports, and historical runs

Confirmed 4,407 unique study rows, a 58-study gold integration set, three-plane decoding, and preliminary—not conclusive—historical signal.

✓

2. Repair and independently validate retrieval metrics

Collapsed embeddings now receive chance-level expected credit. All 18 best checkpoints of the time were re-scored; none contained ties and none changed.

✓

3. Make the report language contract explicit

Pinned multilingual E5 with mean pooling and prefixing. Tokenizer QC covers all 4,407 reports. The language policy has since been measured directly and is null.

✓

4. Finish canonical full-cohort manifest and pixel QC

Complete. Schema, hashes, patient-grouped splits and the translated cohort are frozen, and all 4,407 rows pass three-plane decode/normalization QC. The translated cohort carries 4,405 training-eligible rows: two duplicate-pixel studies are quarantined from it only.

✓

5. Make experiments reproducible and resumable

Protected outputs, exact resume state, model/source revision hashes, compact checkpoints, atomic bounded caching, and training-health diagnostics are implemented and tested. Matrix selection is a one-way door: a written validation-selection.json refuses to be rewritten.

✓

6. Train the full-cohort baseline

Done on an RTX 3070. Validation loss and retrieval behave as expected: training loss falls throughout, validation loss reaches its minimum early, and selection is on validation R@1 rather than loss.

✓

7. Run controlled representation ablations

Report language, adaptation depth, schedule length, text-tower adaptation, pooling at readout, and a shuffled-pairing control — one change at a time, three seeds wherever a claim is made. Summarised above.

8

8. Evaluate on an independent cohort

Still open. The 58 gold cases have now all been examined, including two from the former test split. The other 438 test studies have not been scored here, and repeated use of the gold set limits it as an independent model-selection test.

9

9. Establish usefulness beyond retrieval

The real open work. The older gold-only probe used 56 cases exposed to representation training or selection. New LM-supervised heads were scored on all 58 cases excluded from their fitting, but repeated examination of this small gold cohort remains a limit. External cohort, subgroups, calibration, failure modes and expert review are still needed.

What is still missing or uncertain

Preprocessing: what the pipeline never sees (measured 2026-09-14)

Audited against the DICOM rather than the provided tables, after prev/rsna_knee_101.ipynb flagged the series CSV as unreliable. Full detail: acquisition-metadata-trust.md.

  • The series CSV carries one bit, not two. Fluid_Sensitive is byte-identical to Fat_Suppression on 24,371 of 24,371 rows. pick_study_slots documents itself as preferring fluid-sensitive series and actually selects fat-suppressed ones — but they correlate, so what it picks is 99.9% fluid-sensitive anyway. Right answer, wrong reason; no cohort needs rebuilding.
  • 4.6% of the pixels are read. Studies hold a median of 5 series (range 3–14) and ~194 slices; the pipeline opens 3 series and 9 slices. The discarded series are diagnostic, not localizers — dominated by T1 non-fat-sat, the contrast the model has never seen. Coronal / T1 / no-fatsat fills 64.1% of studies.
  • The three channels of one input straddle a median 62.7mm — wider than the joint. DINOv2 is being fed three anatomically unrelated images stacked as RGB, and 89.9% of slices are never read.
  • The 130mm crop discards real anatomy: it retains 86.1% of tissue on average (p10 70.6%). It could not simply be raised — the old guard silently skipped whenever the box exceeded the image, so scale was never normalised for those series. Crop-or-pad now makes the extent exact; 150mm is chosen for the re-baseline.
  • No detectable protocol leak. Series count predicts none of the 12 gold labels (0 of 12 at p<0.05, mean |AUROC−0.5| 0.058) — though with 58 labelled studies this only rules out effects above ~0.15.
  • Verified sound: slice ordering (filename order matches anatomy in 0 of 180 series; the pipeline sorts geometrically), rescale slope/intercept, and laterality (98.3% resolved, the rest left unmirrored rather than guessed).

Every claim re-verified on this project's data rather than taken from the notebook. None of this has been acted on yet: a preprocessing change invalidates the image cache and makes recorded runs non-comparable, so it needs a new experiment version and a re-baseline.

Limits on the numbers above

  • The levels are development statistics; the generalisation estimate is 0.6938. 56 of 58 gold studies were exposed to training or selection, and the reports state the findings. A held-out replication measured that inflation at −0.0168 and confirmed +0.0500 of real gain on unseen studies. Only levels are affected — paired differences compare arms sharing the same exposure. Write-up.
  • Depth and learning rate are confounded. Every arm used one rate chosen when one block was trainable.
  • Two labels regress at depth (Medial Meniscus, MCL) — the two that unadapted DINOv2 already reads best. With 8–33 positives per label, single-label movements are fragile.
  • Do not tune the probe on these 56 studies. Sweeping C, class weights or fold count would raise macro AUROC by selecting on the evaluation set.
  • Contrastive checkpoint selection used validation retrieval R@1. The later four-tower comparison reads gold AUROC after selection; its observed ranking has not been validated on a new independent gold cohort.

Required before clinical claims

  • Patient independence is unverifiable, not merely unresolved. De-identification re-randomised every identity tag — proven by a known duplicate pair carrying two different PatientIDs on identical pixels. Every figure here is a study-level estimate.
  • Validate multilingual medical semantics, not only tokenizer coverage.
  • Assess the information lost by representing a 3D study with nine slices.
  • Add an external cohort, subgroup analysis, calibration, and expert error review.
  • Handle clinically similar studies that ordinary InfoNCE treats as negatives — measured and closed at this batch size: genuine duplicates appear in ~9% of batches, too rare for either remedy to act on.
