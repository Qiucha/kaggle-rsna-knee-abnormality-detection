Full path to complete contrastive learning

✓

1. Audit pairing, preprocessing, reports, and historical runs

Confirmed 4,407 unique study rows, a clean 58-study translated integration set, three-plane pilot decoding, and preliminary—not conclusive—historical signal.

✓

2. Repair and independently validate retrieval metrics

Collapsed embeddings now receive chance-level expected credit. All 18 current best checkpoints were re-scored; none contained ties and none changed.

✓

3. Make the report language contract explicit

The full experiment uses pinned multilingual E5 with mean pooling and prefixing. Tokenizer QC covers 4,407 reports, although semantic multilingual radiology validation remains open.

4

4. Finish canonical full-cohort manifest and pixel QC

The schema, hashes, patient-grouped splits, and translated pilot artifact are complete. Full three-plane decode/normalization QC is currently running on CPU.

✓

5. Make experiments reproducible and resumable

Protected outputs, exact resume state, model/source revision hashes, compact checkpoints, atomic bounded caching, and training-health diagnostics are implemented and tested.

6

6. Train the projection-only full-cohort baseline

Run 00-projection-full-mean-512 on a CUDA machine. Confirm improving validation loss/retrieval, positive-negative separation, stable variance, temperature, and gradients before doing ablations.

7

7. Run controlled representation ablations

Compare 256 vs 512 tokens, CLS vs masked mean pooling, report sections, image-only unfreezing, text-only unfreezing, and both towers—one controlled change at a time.

8

8. Freeze the choice and evaluate the locked test once

Choose the experiment from validation results, persist the immutable selection, then produce test metrics, bootstrap intervals, and per-query error examples once.

9

9. Establish usefulness beyond retrieval

Evaluate predefined downstream labels, external generalization, clinically meaningful subgroups, calibration, failure modes, and expert review before making performance claims.

What is still missing or uncertain

Required before trusting a model result

  • Complete and inspect full-cohort pixel-QC counts.
  • Train the full projection baseline on an actual CUDA device.
  • Confirm that learning curves beat random ranking with healthy embeddings—not just a lucky selected epoch.
  • Review truncation and section extraction errors for long reports.
  • Keep the test split locked until validation-based selection is frozen.

Required before clinical claims

  • Validate multilingual medical semantics, not only tokenizer coverage.
  • Resolve whether unique anonymized PatientIDs truly represent unique patients.
  • Handle clinically similar/multi-positive studies that ordinary InfoNCE treats as negatives.
  • Assess the information lost by representing a 3D study with only nine slices.
  • Add downstream pathology probes, an external cohort, subgroup analysis, calibration, and expert error review.
