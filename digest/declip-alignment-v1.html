Executive Summary: Transforming Low-Data Radiology Alignment with DeCLIP

Standard CLIP training on paired medical imaging data (such as knee MRIs paired with unstructured radiology reports) suffers from two major bottlenecks: sample inefficiency (only one pair per study) and false-negative penalty (inflicting maximal cross-entropy punishment when two distinct patients share the exact same clinical knee finding, like medial osteoarthritis or meniscal tear). Implementing the DeCLIP (Data-Efficient CLIP) framework based on docs/notes/Training_Guide_on_CLIP.md overcomes these constraints.

Core Clinical Takeaway from DeCLIP Formulation

By connecting each MRI study not only to its verbatim clinical text, but also to its nearest-neighbour report in the rolling semantic queue, DeCLIP resolves the false-negative penalty that degraded rare pathology discrimination at large batch sizes, allowing small cohorts (3,526 studies) to achieve the feature density of datasets an order of magnitude larger.

Technical Formulation & Mathematical Objectives

1. Multi-View Cross-Modal Supervision (L_MV)

For each study-report pair (X_i, R_i), construct augmented MRI view X_i' = Aug_I(X_i) preserving pathology and masked report view R_i' = Aug_T(R_i) preserving clinical semantics:

L_MV = (1/4) * [ L(X,R) + L(X',R) + L(X,R') + L(X',R') ]

Applied via symmetric bidirectional InfoNCE loss across all 4 combinations.

2. Nearest-Neighbour Report Supervision (L_NN)

Maintains a FIFO queue Q_T = {z_j^T} of capacity 512. For each report R_i, retrieves its nearest semantic neighbour:

j* = argmax_{j != i, z_j in Q_T} (z_i^T)^T z_j^T • R_i^NN = R_j*

Adds (X_i, R_i^NN) and (X_i', R_i^NN) as additional positive pairs: L_NN = (1/2)*[L(X, R^NN) + L(X', R^NN)].

3. Total Training Objective (L_total)

L_total = L_MV + λ_NN * L_NN + λ_I * L_I

When backbones are frozen, L_total = L_MV + λ_NN * L_NN optimizes the projection heads g_I and g_T without altering foundation representations. When unfreezing blocks or training LoRA adapters, unimodal image self-supervision L_I (SimCLR between X and X') actively guides the vision tower.
