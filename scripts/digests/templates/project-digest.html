How MRI pixels become the input

  1. Select one fluid-sensitive series per plane and order its DICOM slices geometrically, with deterministic metadata fallbacks.
  2. Sample three slices across the central 20–80% index band. Apply a 130 mm center crop where spacing and dimensions permit.
  3. Clip/rescale using the sampled stack’s 1st–99th percentiles, resize to 224 × 224, and apply the left-knee orientation convention.
  4. During training, apply spatial/intensity augmentation. Apply channel normalization before DINOv2.

The representation sees nine sampled slices per study. Unsampled anatomy and source/report alignment remain limitations.

How learning connects the towers

Illustration with four pairs. Each row retrieves a report from an image; each column retrieves an image from a report. The true study/report pairing sits on the diagonal.

L = ½ [CE(S, diagonal) + CE(Sᵀ, diagonal)]

AdamW · projection/fusion LR 1e-3 · image adaptation LR 1e-5 · weight decay 0.02 on eligible matrix weights · 10% warmup then cosine decay · gradient clip 1.0 · AMP. Learned inverse temperature starts at 1/0.07 and is capped at 100.

Frozen backbones stay in evaluation mode. Image adaptation puts that backbone in training mode while only its selected parameters receive updates.

What the 20 worst candidate pairs revealed

Report-level disagreements persist in meniscal, ligament and cartilage findings. Only five of the 20 direction/query entries exceeded 512 tokens. Some reports contain internal inconsistencies. Truncation is not a complete explanation; no clinical adjudication or relabeling was performed.

Metric meaning: mean R@1 averages image→report and report→image top-1 retrieval with uniform expected tie credit. Random top-1 is 1/441 ≈ 0.227%. Retrieval performance is an intermediate representation check; it is not pathology diagnostic accuracy.
04 / DATA & CONFIDENCE

What is known, and what remains open.

A versioned, quarantined cohort

3,524 train441 validation438 test unscored; 2 gold scored2 quarantined

4,407 original studies remain in the manifest. Two training studies share identical decoded pixels across 87 selected-series slices but have conflicting reports. Both are excluded from training eligibility; source rows and historical runs are preserved.

Integrity and reproducibility

Both completed comparison runs have contiguous 20-epoch histories, verified best-checkpoint hashes and zero exact-reload metric deltas. The image-adaptation execution copies the same frozen code snapshot; all 46 source-inventory hashes were checked.

Manifest SHA-256: 8f6f021954e268b995dbe63daf42a2cc5f0dd92efdd27ad511b0ddbe5832f99b

Prior implementation suite: 37 passed, one CUDA-only test skipped. Both completed arms logged infinite gradient norm at epoch 2 under AMP; later norms and selected metrics were finite. Skipped optimizer-step counts were not recorded.

Evidence boundaries

  • Patient independence is unverifiable, not merely unresolved

    Anonymisation is per study, proven by the quarantined duplicate pair carrying two different PatientID values on identical pixels. No cross-study patient identity can be recovered, so every reported number is a study-level estimate and no future audit can close this.

  • 58 user-identified ground-truth cases

    Other pathology labels are weak supervision. Only eight labeled validation cases exist, including zero positive MCL cases.

  • Report overlap remains

    Twenty exact translated-report groups span splits (78 studies). Matching templates do not establish clinical equivalence.

  • Gold test cases have now been examined

    The two gold-labeled studies in the original test split were scored in the later 58-case LM-head study; the other 438 test studies remain excluded. Earlier gold-only pathology probes fitted on 56 exposed gold studies were development readouts: 48 were contrastive training pairs and 8 selected every checkpoint. The later LM heads trained on pseudo-labels with all 58 gold studies excluded from fitting.
